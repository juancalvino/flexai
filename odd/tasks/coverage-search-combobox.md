## Objective
Make the “Buscá tu localidad” dropdown on `/cobertura` visually consistent with its dark rounded search input while preserving all map/search behavior. Align side-by-side fields in the shared forms and rename the optional phone label exactly as requested.

## Scope
- Replace the browser-native `datalist` with a map-specific, accessible listbox below the search input: same dark palette, rounded surface, restrained accent focus/hover, readable long names, bounded scroll and mobile touch targets. Preserve zone map selection, short partial queries, ambiguous multi-zone feedback, aliases/within mapping, unmatched feedback, reset, panel/chips, mobile summary, and coverage-only payload (no prices). Do not force intake autocomplete's strict three-character validation onto map search.

Keep semantic combobox/listbox ARIA, status feedback and focus.
- In shared `ContactForm`, show exact label `Telefono (Opcional)` instead of `Teléfono adicional / de contacto (opcional)`; keep the phone optional and preserve message behavior. Align paired controls on `/contacto` and `/planes` at desktop widths while retaining mobile stacking, validation/focus and autocomplete overlays. Measure before changing layout; do not invent a defect where geometry already aligns.
- Integrate shared source/tests into `dev` without prices and update `feat/with-plans` with prices, preserving only `src/data/features.ts` as their tree difference. At the user's later explicit request, merge updated `dev` into `main` and push `main` to `origin` after independent verification and a remote-ref safety check; no force-push. Preserve the old pricing branch, backup/stash, OFF `.codegraph/` and other unrelated files; no real contact messages.
